Privacy Policy

bill ueqstions notifier · Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains how the bill ueqstions notifier mobile app (the "App") handles your information. The App is an unofficial, independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bill Wurtz or billwurtz.com.

Short version: the App has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It does not collect, sell or share your personal information. Everything you save stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what the App needs to load billwurtz.com, plus any question you choose to send to that site.

Information stored on your device

The App keeps the following only in local storage on your phone. It is never sent to the developer:

  • A cached copy of the public questions and answers from billwurtz.com, and which entries you have already seen.
  • Questions you add to your "watched" list and whether they have been answered.
  • Entries you star, and your "where I left off" bookmark.
  • The time of the last check for new answers.

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

On Android, the system's own backup feature (Android Auto Backup) may include this app data in your personal device backup if you have backups turned on. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings and the developer has no access to it.

Network requests to billwurtz.com

To show and check for new answers, the App downloads public pages from billwurtz.com, both while you use it and periodically in the background (roughly once an hour, as your operating system allows). Like any website visit, these requests reveal your IP address and basic technical details (such as the request time) to billwurtz.com and its hosting providers. The App does not add any identifiers about you to these requests.

Questions you send to Bill

If you use the "ask bill" feature, the App asks you to confirm and then submits the text of your question to billwurtz.com using the same public form as the website. The App sends only the question text, with no name, account or device identifier. Once submitted, your question is handled by billwurtz.com, not by the App or its developer, and may be published publicly on that site if it is answered. Do not include personal information in questions you send.

Questions you only "watch" without sending stay on your device. The App matches them against new answers locally.

Notifications

The App asks for permission to show notifications. All notifications are created locally on your device after it checks billwurtz.com. The App does not use a push notification server and does not create or send any push token or device identifier. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Links and clipboard

Tapping a link in an answer opens it in your browser or another app. Those websites have their own privacy policies. If you choose "copy link", the App places that link on your clipboard. The App does not read your clipboard.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load billwurtz.com and submit questions you choose to send.
  • Notifications: to tell you about new answers and answered questions.
  • Run at startup / wake lock / vibrate: to keep the background check scheduled after a reboot, finish a check, and vibrate for notifications.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Information we do not collect

The developer runs no servers for the App and receives no data from it. The App includes no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not sell or share any personal information with third parties.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information, there is nothing for the developer to access or delete.

Changes to this policy

If the App's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact
